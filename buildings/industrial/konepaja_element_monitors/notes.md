# konepaja_element_monitors

## Style brief

A Nordic machinery works (*konepaja*: an engineering works, a machine shop)
of the 1970s-80s, built of precast concrete elements: one building on a
32 x 24 m plan, long side to the street. It brings the precast era and a
non-brick look into a catalog whose industry is otherwise mid-century brick.
A generic composite of the type, not a portrait of any one works.

- **The production hall, 32 x 16 m, at the back:** exposed-aggregate wall
  elements 2.67 m wide and full height, a smooth cast column cover over
  every other joint (the 5.33 m column grid), a darker cast plinth, and a
  deep dark profiled-sheet fascia from 7.5 to 8.5 m round a flat felt roof.
  One high window in every bay, 4 m of steel lights at 5.0-6.0 m, otherwise
  blank: a hall reads by its tall closed walls.
- **Its roof is the building's identity from above:** three long rooflight
  monitors on the column lines, 10.7 m apart, each 2.5 m wide, 1.25 m high
  and 12.5 m long, their glazing leaning in from a sheeted upstand to a
  dark sheet top, stopping 1.75 m short of the fascia at both ends; two rows
  of opal rooflight domes between them; an air handler, three extract fans,
  and in the back west corner a guyed black steel flue to 15.25 m, open at
  the top, a ladder painted up it.
- **The office nose, 13.3 x 8 m, at the west street corner:** two storeys,
  7.1 m to its fascia, in smooth pale panels with one ribbon of steel
  lights to each storey, its glazed entrance and stair bay at its east end
  under a cantilevered canopy, KONEPAJA in plain pale capitals on its
  fascia.
- **The staff and stores strip, 18.7 x 8 m, east of it:** single-storey,
  4.1 m to its fascia, in the hall's aggregate elements, two windows and a
  stores door to the street.
- **The truck door** is on the hall's east wall close behind the strip: a
  3.3 x 3.9 m sectional door with a row of small lights, and a personnel
  door beyond it. A truck drives in from the street along the east side,
  stands beside the door and backs out; nothing of the building stands on
  the ground on that side, and the strip's fascia runs flush there.
- What makes it a machinery works rather than a warehouse or an office: the
  monitors and domes, the flue and the fans, the step from the tall blank
  hall to the lower glazed office, and few, large doors.

## Category and secondary use

`buildings/industrial/`, a machine shop. Secondary use: **offices** (the
office nose, both storeys) and **staff rooms and stores** (the strip).

## Hero/landmark status

Not a hero. Ordinary triangle ceilings and the 1024 texture cap apply, and all
are met.

## Construction

Closed solids unioned with Blender's exact boolean (`asset_build.union`):

1. **three bodies**, each a box from the ground to its deck: the hall (deck
   8.25 m), the office (6.875 m) and the strip (3.875 m), set face to face
   (the office and the strip against the hall at y = -4, the strip against
   the office at x = -2.667). Each wall is built in horizontal zones, each
   zone one UV mapping, and every opening reaching a zone is a hole, a notch
   from its foot or head, or a cut right through it, its outline carrying
   every breakpoint the zone's neighbours put on it. Openings are reveals
   round a closing plane split into its lights and modules on the cut
   tiers, flat quads on the flush tiers. A ground face notched at every
   recess that stands on the ground closes each box;
2. **three fascia rings**: the hall's all round, the office's a U open to
   the hall, the strip's an L open to the hall and the office and flush with
   the east wall; each from its eave to 0.25 m over its deck, 0.094 m proud
   of the panels and 0.3125 m in over them, which makes the roof's upstand;
3. **the rooflights**: three monitors and eight domes, each sunk 0.1 m into
   the deck;
4. **the plant**: the flue, its three guys, the air handler, three fans
   (body and cowl), each sunk 0.1 m into the deck;
5. **the canopy**, sunk 0.25 m into the office front and through the
   entrance bay's glazing, and **the landing step** against the plinth.

The union leaves the boundary of one empty volume; the touching faces
between the bodies go. The union snaps every operand's vertices to a
0.1 mm grid first (`asset_build.union(..., snap=asset_build.SNAP)`): the
office and the strip meet at x = -2.667 m, which float arithmetic left a
hair off coplanar from one solid to the other, so the exact boolean kept
both faces as a 62 m2 internal double wall until the vertices were
snapped. A 0.1 mm weld and a limited dissolve (0.001 rad, UV
seams kept) follow on every tier. Then **the lean kit** goes in, appended
rather than unioned (`roof_safety.append(..., check=True)`, which fails if a
piece touches the building or another piece): the wall ladder and its cage
(`roof_safety.WallLadder(..., lean=True)`, `Cage`) on the hall's west wall.
The plinth and the column covers are painted flush, so the ladder takes no
`behind`. After the weld every tier is checked for any vertex outside the
east wall's plane under the strip's eave (3.5 m); the build fails if one is
there.

Every number the mesh and the atlas share is in `facade_atlas.py`: the plan
(HX = 16, HY = 12, the hall's front at y = -4, BAY = 5.333, the office's
east wall at -16 + 2.5 BAY), every height, the window and door sizes, the
entrance bay's modules, the monitors', domes' and plant's positions and
sizes. `build.py` keeps the elevations' opening lists and `check_layout()`,
which fails the build if:

- an opening comes within 0.4 m of a corner or 0.3 m of another, or an edge
  of one lies on a zone line;
- the office's storey leaves 3.0-3.3 m, a personnel leaf leaves 2.0-2.4 m,
  the entrance has no transom over its leaves, a ribbon's sill leaves desk
  height, the upper ribbon runs into the fascia, or a ribbon comes within
  0.3 m of the band change;
- the hall's windows come within 0.4 m of the strip's fascia, a monitor
  leaves the column lines or comes within 1.2 m of the fascia;
- any piece of the roof (monitor, dome, fan, air handler, flue, a guy's
  foot) comes within 0.3 m of the fascia, 0.4 m of another or of an outlet;
- the flue is not 6-8 m over the fascia or not in the back west corner;
- the canopy does not cover the entrance bay within the office, crowds the
  ribbons, misses the spandrel or does not cover the landing;
- the truck door comes within 0.6 m of the hall's corner;
- the ladder or its cage crowds a corner or a window of the west wall
  (`roof_safety.problems()`).

### The bays

The 16 m wrap of the AGG band holds three bays: a column cover on every bay
line, two panels to a bay, one hall window in the middle of each. Every hall
wall is a whole number of bays (32 m = 6, 16 m = 3) and starts its band on a
bay line, so the covers stand at every corner and the windows sit in every
bay. The strip's front starts mid-bay so its covers stay on the hall's
column lines (x = 0, 5.33, 10.67, 16); its east wall starts on one at the
street corner. The office's width, 2.5 bays, puts its east wall mid-bay,
which is why the hall's wall over the strip carries no windows: its band is
sampled 3.375 m lower there, so only plain panel rows reach it.

### The monitors

Each monitor's long sides are a sheeted upstand 0.25 m high, then glazing
leaning in 0.45 m over 1.0 m to a 1.6 m flat top in the fascia's sheet: the
glass shows from above and lights there at night, which a vertical side did
not. Ten 1.25 m lights a side, each its own face on one region to 70 m; one
face on a DPPM region with the lights painted past it.

### The flue

An octagon 0.6 m across the flats from the deck to 15.25 m, 6.75 m over the
hall's fascia, at (-13.8, 9.25), open at the top round a flue sunk 1.0 m and
sooted, a rolled rim and a guy collar 3 cm proud up close (both painted on
every tier), three guy rods from the collar at 12.75 m to anchor plates in
the deck 1.6 m out. Its faces sample one region by height and by the way
round, so the painted ladder up three faces keeps its place on six or four
sides. Octagonal, after the plant hall's stub stacks, rather than
`asset_build.open_stack()`, which makes a square stack.

### The entrance

A 2.5 m glazed bay at the office front's east end, from the threshold to
6.03 m, 0.2 m deep, in four modules (doors, transom and spandrel, stair
glazing twice); the canopy at 3.03-3.28 m over it on the first floor's
line; one step up (0.156 m), so no handrail.

### Rainwater

Internal drainage: six outlets painted in the decks (four on the hall's,
clear of the monitors and the plant, one on the office's, one on the
strip's), with ponding stains round them. No gutter, so no downpipe.

## Glazing treatment

`glazing.rooms()` on the finished albedo: measured by the validator at
**sRGB 57 (linear 0.037)** in every variant, 0.27-0.29x the surrounding
trim. ORM roughness `glazing.ROUGHNESS` (0.08), metallic 0. Inside the
Style Direction band; no departure. Frames are the period's dark brown
(`grey_aggregate`), orange-red (`beige_aggregate`) or natural aluminium
(`red_aggregate`), the monitors' always aluminium; the glass is darker than
every one. The domes' opal shells are not glass: light, opaque, roughness
0.30. They are in the emission mask (Night lighting), so the validator's
glass measure takes their pixels in with the panes' (about 2% of the mask);
the panes alone are inside the band with room to spare.

## Variant list

A mid-size monolithic works, so one coherent cladding scheme per instance:
the aggregate, its column covers, the fascia (with the monitors' and the
ladder's sheet), the frames, the office's panels and the doors change
together; the plinth, the felt, the glass, the flue and the plant are fixed.
Three aggregate schemes rather than a smooth white third: smooth panels on
the hall would change the surface pattern, which would need its own ORM and
normal map, and the brief asked for honesty there.

- **`grey_aggregate`** (default): grey granite aggregate, dark brown
  fascia, dark brown frames and doors, off-white office panels.
- **`beige_aggregate`**: beige gravel aggregate, rust-red fascia, orange-red

## Night lighting

- **Mask:** `konepaja_element_monitors_emission.png`, shared by every tier
  and colour: every window light (the hall's in the AGG band, so one mask
  serves the cut and the flat tiers), the monitors' glazing, the entrance
  bay, the truck door's lights and the personnel doors' vision panels, each
  pane painted neutral grey by `glazing.night_rooms()`; and the **domes'
  opal shells**, an even neutral 150/255 (sRGB), a third of a pane's
  level in linear light, as a diffuser over a lit shop floor reads. From
  above, the lit monitors and domes are the works at night. Frames, walls,
  fascias, the roof and the lettering are black: nothing else glows.
- **Reference lit factor: linear (1.0, 0.92, 0.80)**: a workshop under
  fluorescent and high-bay lamps, a little cooler than the catalog's
  offices.
- **Default off:** `emissiveFactor [0, 0, 0]` in all four tiers, the
  `emissiveTexture` binding intact.
- **Control:** one factor per placed building. Each kind of light shares
  one region (the hall's windows share a band), so no window, and not the
  office alone, can be lit by itself.
- **`variants.png`:** the three colours at `LOD0`, a day row (emission off)
  above a night row at the reference factor, by
  `scripts/variant_previews.py`, camera, framing, exposure and colour
  management fixed. Light on the ground in the night row is the preview's
  bounce, **simulated**; spill is the consumer's.
- **Pending in the consumer:** off/on behaviour on reimported tiers, distant
  filtering under emission, LOD switches when lit.

## Export

`.glb` + external PNGs through `scripts/asset_build.py`:
`export_format='GLB'`, `use_selection=True`, `export_yup=True`,
`export_apply=True`, `export_keep_originals=True`, `export_cameras=False`,
`export_lights=False`. Emission is exported lit and the factor rewritten to
zero. `strip_embedded_images()` removes the placeholder `bufferView`s.

Checked by the validator on all four tiers:

- one mesh, one material slot;
- `baseColorTexture` → `konepaja_element_monitors_albedo_grey_aggregate.png`;
- `metallicRoughnessTexture` and `occlusionTexture` → `..._orm.png`;
- `normalTexture` → `..._normal.png`, scale 1, no tangents;
- `emissiveTexture` → `..._emission.png`, `emissiveFactor [0, 0, 0]`;
- every image a `uri`, no `bufferView`.

Pivot: ground level, centre of `LOD0`'s plan bounds, applied to every tier.
Front faces `-Y` in Blender, `+Z` in the exported glTF frame.

The facts sidecar `export/konepaja_element_monitors.model.json` lists:

- **footprint:** the 32 x 24 m outer wall faces; the fascias' oversail, the
  canopy, the landing step and the caged ladder stand outside it;
- **storeys:** two: the ground floor over the whole plan, 768 m2, and the
  office's first floor at 3.4375 m, 106.67 m2;
- **dwellings:** 0;
- **doors:** `entrance` (main, kind entrance, office front, the pair
  1.625 x 2.1875 m clear, threshold 0.156 m), `loading` (kind vehicle, the
